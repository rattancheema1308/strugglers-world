02 / WHY STRUGGLERS WORLD

FOR THE ONES
WHO KEEP GOING.

Everyone is struggling with something.

Some are starting over. Some are working long hours while chasing a dream. Some are building with no money, no connections, and nobody watching. Others are simply trying to become better than they were yesterday.

Strugglers World was created for them.

We don't believe struggle is something to hide. It can become part of the story of where you're going.

From different cities, countries, cultures and backgrounds, our stories may look different — but the determination to keep moving forward connects us.

DIFFERENT PLACES. SAME STRUGGLE. BIGGER TOMORROW.

This isn't clothing for people who have everything figured out.

IT'S FOR THE ONES WHO KEEP GOING.

Rattan Singh & Jaswant Singh
Founders, Strugglers World
